# T017 independent timing check: pre-fit design

Codex (GPT-6.1-sol), 9 November 2026. This design and its JSON case list are
written before this implementation opens a light curve. Parent review and a
commit of `configs/independent_validation/t017_design.json` are required before
the extraction command is allowed to run. All outcomes remain exploratory;
the frozen measurement, calibration and evaluation are retained unchanged.

Only existing local products from S46–S73 or S98 onward can be opened.

saved metadata. Missing files or mismatched hashes remain explicit failures.

## Independent method

Use the standard Astropy BoxLeastSquares implementation to search phase
without an issued epoch. Its common library with Claude's checker is disclosed;
the extraction, detrending, timing model and profiling code are independently
written here. Search 20,000 logarithmically spaced periods from0.3–15days,
with durations0.03,0.05,0.08,0.12,0.18,0.24days, masking up to three sequential
signals. Assign the target by period alone, within5% of its catalogue period.
Retain all detected signals and match/alias ambiguity, rather than forcing a
match to the expected phase.

The continuum is a piecewise-linear interpolation of half-day80th-percentile
flux bins within contiguous segments. The timing model is a symmetric empirical
trapezoid, distinct from the frozen and Claude batman models. Fit its shared
duration/ingress shape from the target's folded signal; per-transit time uses
the raw normalised flux and independently profiled linear baseline and depth.
Search a window2.5durations wide, capped at0.45period per side, without using
the issued forecast. Require at least eight out-of-transit points on both
sides, and explicitly retain coverage/edge/non-detection failures. Repeat on
PDCSAP and SAP as shared-photon reduction sensitivities, not independent data.

For every influential event whose frozen result is non-Gaussian, additionally
fit local depth/duration/ingress jointly with time and baseline. This is a
bounded nuisance-parameter sensitivity, not full posterior marginalisation or
a newly qualified timing pipeline. Use a15-second profile grid for conditional
timing uncertainty. Record model mismatch, multiple modes and failed fits.

## Discrepancy rules fixed before fitting

- Product review: median absolute timing difference exceeds the greater of
  three minutes and half the median frozen sigma; any event exceeds the
  greater of ten minutes and three frozen sigmas; over10% of frozen accepted
  windows are unmatched; or aliases/companions leave target identity ambiguous.
- Shape review: influential non-Gaussian timing shifts from a joint local shape
  fit exceed the greater of three minutes and half a frozen sigma.
- Wider remeasurement is warranted by a coherent discrepant influential train,
  two large discrepancies in one influential product, or two controls passing
  product review. Report this fixed sample completely first. A broader tranche
  requires an explicit scientific reconciliation; there is no automatic
  enlargement, threshold relaxation or replacement of frozen results.

The triggers are diagnostics on shared photons, not calibrated significance
tests. Comparison matching happens only after independent fitting. Non-detection,
ambiguity and failure of a trapezoid model do not identify an ephemeris miss.
No pixel/centroid analysis is included; unresolved identity remains unresolved.

## C050 interpretation to retain during this check

Opposite b/c residual signs and an approximately on-time d constrain a single
shared clock offset. They are not, alone, a measured dynamical anticorrelation
or proof against every target-specific extraction/identification error.
PDCSAP and SAP share photons, and both checkers share the source photometry;
their agreement supports robustness across implementations, not observational
independence. Physical TTV attribution should remain supported-but-qualified
until those limitations are separated from the independently reproduced train.
