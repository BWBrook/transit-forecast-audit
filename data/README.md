# Data organisation

- `templates/`: header-only production ledgers plus a provisional pilot queue.
- `fixtures/`: small attributed published benchmark targets. These are not our
  computed results and are not individual transit measurements.
- `raw/`: original acquired artifacts, kept immutable and outside normal Git
  tracking (gitignored except its README). It holds the ExoClock DRB, DRC and
  DRD release files from OSF (`raw/osf/`), public metadata caches, and
  development-era TESS products from MAST (`raw/mast/`, sectors 46-73 only).
  Hashes are in `results/pilot_v1/source_manifest.csv` and the product tables
  under `results/`. Nothing dated after 2025-11-18 may be requested or opened
  before a protocol freeze.
- `derived/`: normalised or analysed tables regenerated by the scripts
  (gitignored except its README), for example `derived/sprint1/` and
  `derived/outcomes/`. The sprint-1 bulk archive
  (`results/sprint1/bulk_archive_receipt.json`) can restore its contents.

Real ExoClock release files and development-era TESS light curves have been
downloaded and analysed; see `docs/agent_guide.md` for the data flow. Synthetic
demonstration outputs live under `results/seed_demo/` and are identified as
artificial in their contents.

See `docs/data_contract.md` for semantics before writing adapters.
